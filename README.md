# 🚀 Cyber-Cafe-SaaS

### Smart • Secure • Scalable Cyber Cafe Management Platform

A modern full-stack SaaS platform designed to simplify and modernize cyber cafe operations. Cyber-Cafe-SaaS provides powerful administration tools, secure document management, digital signatures, audit tracking, and cafe discovery through a unified platform.

🌐 **Live Demo:** [Visit Cyber-Cafe-SaaS](https://teri-netlify-site.netlify.app/)

---

## 📌 Overview

Cyber-Cafe-SaaS is designed to help cyber cafe owners manage their operations efficiently through a centralized digital platform.

The platform focuses on simplifying management workflows, improving document security, maintaining operational transparency, and providing a better experience for both administrators and users.
